# SKU Commercialization Scorecard

Status: manager decision tool
Date: 2026-10-29

Scores are internal judgement, not market data. 1 = weak / difficult, 5 = strong / favorable.

## Weighting
- Client adaptability × 2
- Case-study value × 2
- Manufacturability × 1.5
- Repeatability across clients × 1.5
- Visual impact × 1
- Interaction uniqueness × 1
- Packaging value × 1
- Sample cost efficiency × 1
- Lead-time efficiency × 1
- Margin potential × 1

## Active commercial proof set
Only these three may consume P0 sample/procurement attention:

1. **SIGNAL KEY SET** — interaction / hardware / software proof
2. **FLOW BLOCK** — material / collectible / premium packaging proof
3. **PATCH / LABEL SYSTEM** — manufacturing / QC / fulfillment proof

## Kill / replacement rule
No new active SKU enters P0 unless it replaces one of the above and demonstrates a materially higher commercial proof value.

## Why SYNC OBJECT is not P0
It is strategically strong but hardware/audio/light development can expand quickly. SIGNAL KEY provides a more distinctive story with a narrower first prototype.

## Why DROP FLOW is not P0
The moving-flow idea is visually powerful, but FLOW BLOCK can prove material language and premium-object positioning with much less mechanical and reliability risk.

## Why PATCH stays P0
It is intentionally less spectacular. Its job is to create undeniable evidence that VIRTURA can prepare artwork, quote, sample, revise, QC, package and hold inventory.

## Manager rule
The collection is not a democracy. Products remain active because they prove a capability or create repeatable client value, not because they are aesthetically interesting.
