# FLOW BLOCK — Material Sample Execution Pack

Status: P0 commercial proof
Owner: A2 Industrial / A7 Supply Chain
Object: OBJ-007 FLOW BLOCK

## Objective
Produce one premium material sample that proves VIRTURA can turn the idea of “preserved flow” into a believable collectible object with credible manufacturing and packaging potential.

## First-sample rule
Do not start with pumps, circulating liquid, complex mechanics or custom electronics.

The first sample must test:
- optical quality
- internal flow language
- edge finishing
- weight / hand feel
- packaging value
- photographic value

## Candidate processes
### Route A — cast clear resin
Pros: internal bubbles/inclusions can become part of the design; easier small-batch experimentation.
Risks: yellowing, shrinkage, trapped bubbles, inconsistent polish.

### Route B — machined / bonded acrylic
Pros: predictable geometry, cleaner optical edges, easier supplier comparison.
Risks: bonding seams, internal form less organic.

### Route C — hybrid clear body + internal insert
Pros: separates shell quality from the “flow” form; easiest to iterate.
Risks: may look assembled rather than monolithic.

## Sample dimensions
Start around:
- width 110–140 mm
- height 65–90 mm
- depth 28–45 mm

Final dimensions must be set only after supplier feasibility and shipping/packaging review.

## Internal visual concepts to quote
Quote at least two:
1. frozen wave / curved transparent insert
2. suspended bubble / droplet field
3. layered refractive film / optical interface

Avoid generic decorative glitter or colored resin.

## Finish target
- transparent / smoke-clear / neutral only
- optically clean front and top faces
- deliberately controlled side/bottom finish
- no visible cheap glue residue
- no sharp edges
- no decorative RGB

## RFQ questions
- material grade
- casting or machining method
- maximum bubble tolerance
- polishing method
- bonding method
- yellowing resistance
- achievable optical clarity
- unit variance
- sample and tooling fees
- quote at 5 / 20 / 50 / 100 units

## Sample acceptance
Reject if:
- primary viewing face has distracting scratches
- adhesive seam becomes the dominant feature
- internal form reads as generic souvenir resin
- object feels too light / toy-like without intentionality
- polishing is inconsistent across edges
- packaging protection would require excessive foam volume
