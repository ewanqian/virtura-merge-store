# Compliance / Risk Boundary

Product-risk checklist; not legal advice.

## P0 Prototype
Allowed:
- fake prices / fake checkout
- Three.js digital objects
- test accounts
- Conflux testnet
- wallet-connect experiment
- testnet Object Passport

Do not ship in P0:
- real-money purchase of on-chain assets
- investment / appreciation framing
- floor-price mechanics
- open secondary market
- RWA / tokenized physical ownership
- public claim of compliance without professional review

## Public-commerce gates
Review before paid launch:
- operating legal entity
- ordinary e-commerce obligations
- RMB payment provider
- privacy / personal information
- user agreement / digital-content terms
- domain / ICP requirements as applicable
- whether blockchain information service obligations are triggered
- whether a chain/platform partner covers parts of KYC/filing/compliance
